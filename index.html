Our Story
A Legacy of Energy

From a small idea in Thailand to a global phenomenon.

1984
The Discovery

Dietrich Mateschitz discovers Krating Daeng in Thailand and envisions bringing the energy drink to the Western world.

1987
The Launch

Red Bull Energy Drink is officially sold for the first time in Austria, creating an entirely new product category.

2004
F1 Racing

Red Bull acquires the Jaguar Racing F1 team, marking the birth of Red Bull Racing and a new era in motorsport.

2012
Stratos

Felix Baumgartner jumps from the edge of space at 128,100 ft, breaking the sound barrier in freefall.

2025
Global Icon

Red Bull sells over 12 billion cans annually across 172+ countries, powering athletes and dreamers worldwide.
